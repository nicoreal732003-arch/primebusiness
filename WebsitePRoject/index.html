About Us

Prime Business and Collection Services, Inc. (PBCSI) was formally established on January 23, 2025, as an expansion of Executive Collection and Credit Management Services, Inc. (ECCMSI). The creation of PBCSI was driven by the continuous growth of industry demand and the need to strengthen the group’s presence in the collection and credit management sector. As part of the Asialink Group of Companies, and carrying the same principles of professionalism, integrity, and operational excellence upheld by ECCMSI, PBCSI is strategically positioned to provide efficient and reliable collection services. Its coverage extends to Visayas, Mindanao, North Luzon, Central Luzon, and the National Capital Region, ensuring comprehensive support to clients across key regions of the country. With its formal registration and establishment, Prime Business and Collection Services, Inc. is committed to building upon the reputation of ECCMSI as a trusted partner in credit management, while contributing to sustainable growth and financial stability for its stakeholders.
